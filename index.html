FAQ

Extension
How do I start?

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store, sign in with Telegram (one tap, no password), pick a mode in the popup and upload your video as usual.

Which devices does it work on?

Chrome on PC, and browsers that support extensions on iPhone and Android.

Why is there a credit line in my caption?

Posts made with the tool get a short credit line at the end of the caption (Upload Method > @slerbo.edits). You can edit your post afterwards if you like.

Security and account
Are my videos stored anywhere?

No. Your videos are not kept by Slerbo. The extension only checks your account and plan.

Why Telegram sign in?

It is quick, needs no password and keeps your plan tied to you on every device.

Premium
How do I get Premium?

Press Get Premium in the extension or message @slerbo_freefire on Telegram. Your plan is activated on your account right after payment.

Is Slerbo affiliated with TikTok?

No. Slerbo Enhancer is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or any other platform.
