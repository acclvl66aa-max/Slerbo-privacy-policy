FAQ

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Every mode can be tried for free. Premium adds more uploads a day and bigger files.

Which devices does it work on?

Chrome on PC, and browsers that support extensions on iPhone and Android.

Are my videos stored anywhere?

No. Your videos are not kept by Slerbo. The extension only checks your account and plan.

Why is there a credit line in my caption?

Posts made with the tool get a short credit line at the end of the caption (Upload Method > @slerbo.edits). You can edit your post afterwards if you like.

Is it affiliated with TikTok?

No. Slerbo Enhancer is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or any other platform.
