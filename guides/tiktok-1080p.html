How to upload TikTok videos in 1080p

You exported a sharp 1080p edit, posted it, and TikTok plays it at 720p or worse. Here is what usually causes it and what you can do about it.

Why TikTok shows a lower resolution

TikTok makes several copies of every video at different sizes and picks one for each viewer. When the upload is heavy, unusual or comes from the phone app with data saving on, the 1080p copy is often missing or only shown to some viewers.

Export settings that help

  • Resolution: 1080×1920 for vertical edits.
  • Frame rate: 30 or 60 fps. Avoid odd rates like 50 or 25.
  • Codec: H.264 (MP4). Keep the bitrate reasonable: very large files are compressed harder.
  • Length: short edits keep their quality more easily than long ones.

Upload from a computer

Posting from TikTok on the web avoids the app's own re-compression on the phone and data saving. This is the method Slerbo Enhancer builds on.

Turn on high-quality uploads in the app

If you post from your phone, open Settings and privacy, then Data Saver, and make sure it is off. Under the upload screen, keep "Allow high-quality uploads" on.

Check the result

After posting, open the video on another device or ask a friend. Quality can take a few minutes to settle while TikTok finishes processing.
