How to post 60 FPS edits on TikTok

High-fps edits look fluid in your editor, then feel choppy on TikTok. Most videos are played back at 30 fps, so smooth motion needs a little care.

Why your edit drops to 30 fps

TikTok's standard copies of a video are usually 30 fps. A 60 fps file is converted, and half the frames are dropped, which makes fast motion look less smooth.

Export for smooth motion

  • Frame rate: export at exactly 60 fps (or 120 fps if your tool supports it).
  • Motion blur: a little motion blur keeps movement smooth even at 30 fps.
  • Resolution: 1080p is enough. Higher sizes make the file heavier.

Post with a high-fps method

Slerbo Enhancer's Hyper FPS mode is built for 60 and 120 fps edits and keeps every frame. Good to know: with Hyper FPS the video's length on TikTok shows longer than your export, which is how the method keeps the frames.

Check it on a phone

Open the posted video on the TikTok app and watch the fastest part of the edit. That is where frame drops are easiest to see.
