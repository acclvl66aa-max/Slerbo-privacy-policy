Best export settings for TikTok edits

Good quality on TikTok starts with the export. These are the settings that work best for edits, whatever app you use.

The short version

  • Size: 1080×1920 (vertical) or 1920×1080 (landscape).
  • Frame rate: 30 or 60 fps.
  • Format: MP4, H.264.
  • Bitrate: about 10 to 20 Mbps for 1080p. Higher rarely helps on TikTok.
  • Audio: AAC, 44.1 or 48 kHz.

CapCut

Choose 1080p, set the frame rate to 30 or 60, and use the recommended bitrate. Turn on "Smart HDR" only if your clip was filmed in HDR.

Alight Motion

Export as video, 1080p, the same frame rate as your project, and a high quality setting. Avoid exporting at a different frame rate than the project.

After Effects

Render through Media Encoder with the H.264 format, a matching frame rate, VBR 2-pass and a target around 15 Mbps.
